D65 incremental update patch
=============================

Apply over the existing D64/D63 site project. This is not a full website package.

Files included:
- index.html
- styles.css
- product-viewer.js
- assets/section-2-sage-cream-4k.png
- assets/section-3-apricot-cream-4k.png
- assets/section-4-forest-glow-4k.png

Changes:
- Products (section 2): approved 4K sage/cream gradient.
- Ethics (section 3): approved 4K apricot/cream gradient.
- Contact (section 4): approved 4K forest-glow gradient.
- Adds separate browser-generated grain overlays to sections 2–4.
- Increases grain contrast by approximately 25% on the hero and section overlays.
- Removes the product kicker and styles the title in the hero headline's single-weight treatment.
- Adds a right-side arrow to transition between The Usual and Early Doors with a slide/fade animation.
- Early Doors temporarily reuses the existing coffee bag GLB; the product copy and index update.
- Preserves D63 render synchronisation, D62 bag angle, D61 optics, and all current model assets.

Installation:
Extract into the root of the current project and overwrite index.html, styles.css, and product-viewer.js. Add the three PNGs to assets/. No model files need replacing.
